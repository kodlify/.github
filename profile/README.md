## 1. About Kodlify

**Kodlify** is a sports technology and software development studio. It designs, builds and runs software for **sports clubs, academies, facilities, coaches and sports-tech founders**. Sport is the only industry it works in.

Kodlify takes projects from the first conversation to a live product: **discovery, design, development, launch and ongoing support**. Its work covers booking systems, club and academy management tools, websites, iOS and Android apps, and custom platforms for operators who need something that does not exist off the shelf.

**Why a sport-only focus?** Sports businesses run on courts, timetables, coaches and members, and generic business software rarely fits. A padel club needs open matches and Americano nights; an academy needs term enrolments, levels and parent updates; a golf club runs on its tee sheet. Working only in sport means Kodlify already understands these workflows before the first call.

**Design principles ("we build around how your club actually operates"):**

1. **Start with your day-to-day**: who books, who pays, who coaches and who answers the phone. The real workflow is mapped before any screen is designed.
2. **Design for staff and members**: front-desk teams need speed; members need something simple on their phone.
3. **Keep it easy to run**: clear admin tools, sensible defaults and no unused features, so the team can manage it without calling Kodlify.

**Primary focus:** padel. Kodlify also builds for tennis, pickleball, football, golf and basketball, and the same approach works for most court, pitch and class-based sports.

### 5.1 Court Booking Systems

*Custom Court Booking System Development for Sports Clubs and Facilities.* A court booking system lets players reserve and pay for courts, pitches or bays online in real time, while staff run the same calendar from a back office. Built from scratch for racquet clubs, multi-court facilities, leisure centres, unmanned venues and founders. **No commission**: the club pays only its payment provider.

**What it includes**

- **Real-time availability**: each slot is locked the moment someone starts paying, so two people can never take the same court.
- **Rules by court, time and membership**: member booking windows, junior restrictions, limits on prime-time slots.
- **Recurring and block bookings**: season-long team slots, coaching blocks, corporate leagues, invoiced on schedule.
- **Waitlists, cancellations, no-shows**: cancellation windows with refunds or credits, waitlist offers, deposits, repeat no-show rules.
- **Pricing engine**: peak/off-peak pricing, **dynamic pricing**, **split payments** (e.g. a padel four each paying a share), credits, passes and deposits.
- **Unmanned facilities**: booking-driven **door/gate access** (code or QR valid only for the slot), **lights that follow the calendar**, staff alerts for forced doors or overstays. Compatible with interface-enabled hardware such as Nuki or Salto locks (confirmed during scoping).
- **One engine for every space**: courts, football pitches (whole or split), sports halls, golf bays, swimming lanes; overlapping spaces block each other automatically.
- **Website and app booking**: booking flow built into the club's own site, with an optional [club app](#54-sports-club-mobile-apps).

**Integrations:** Stripe plus local methods (SEPA direct debit in Germany/Italy, Bizum in Spain, iDEAL in the Netherlands, regional gateways in the UAE); marketplace listings (Playtomic, MATCHi) with shared availability where allowed; accounting (Xero, QuickBooks, Sage, DATEV); WhatsApp Business API and SMS.

**Switching:** members, future bookings, history and credits are exported, imported and run in parallel with the old system until players are set up.

**"ClubSpark alternative" comparison:** custom build vs booking platforms (ClubSpark, CourtReserve, Playtomic) vs white-label SaaS, covering upfront cost, monthly cost, commission, data/code ownership, branding, roadmap control and migration. See [Section 12](#12-own-vs-rent-alternatives-index).

### 5.2 Club and Facility Management Software

*Bespoke Sports Club Management Software: your club's own platform, not a subscription.* One system for members, fees, bookings, communication and reports, built for multi-sport and racquet clubs, leisure centres, federations and volunteer-run clubs that have outgrown spreadsheets or per-member fees.

**Modules**

- **Membership and CRM**: tiers/categories (playing, social, junior, student, off-peak), family accounts, juniors auto-moved to adult membership, automatic renewals, pro-rata joining fees, waiting lists, notes/tags/segments.
- **Billing and payments (0% platform fee)**: Direct Debit (GoCardless in the UK), SEPA, cards and wallets via Stripe, cash/transfers/Bizum, instalments, automatic retries for failed payments.
- **Bookings and facilities**: member booking rights, classes and sessions, multi-sport facility management with staff rotas and occupancy reports. Built on the same engine as the [court booking system](#51-court-booking-systems).
- **Access control**: clubhouse and gate access tied to membership status (lapsed = access stops), guest day passes, time-limited contractor codes, floodlights tied to access.
- **Bar, shop and accounting (POS)**: member tabs, pro-shop sales, stock, end-of-day reports; sync to Xero, QuickBooks, Sage; DATEV-ready exports for German e.V. clubs.
- **Communications**: email, SMS, push and **WhatsApp Business** (approved templates, opt-in, per-channel consent, messages logged per member).
- **Website + membership as one deliverable**: one login, one domain, content migrated from the old site.
- **Compliance**: GDPR-oriented junior records linked to guardians; safeguarding certificates (e.g. DBS) with expiry reminders.
- **Multi-site clubs and federations**: per-site prices/staff/reports, central registrations, licence fees collected once.

**Alternatives compared:** Pitchero, Spond, ClubRight, white-label SaaS. See [Section 12](#12-own-vs-rent-alternatives-index).

### 5.3 Academy and Coach Management

*Custom Sports Academy Management Software: enrolment, sessions, fees and a parent app.* Replaces spreadsheets and group chats with one system for registrations, scheduling, coaches, fees and parents. Built for football, tennis, swimming, gymnastics and martial-arts academies, coaching businesses, camps and multi-branch franchises. **No fee per athlete.**

**Capabilities**

- **Enrolment and registration**: terms, blocks, drop-ins, trials that convert, waitlists, early-bird prices, family accounts, sibling discounts.
- **Session and coach scheduling**: coach calendars with qualification matching, attendance on the coach's phone, coach hours/payroll, sessions that reserve courts/pitches/lanes.
- **Fees and local payments**: term/monthly fees by Direct Debit, SEPA or card, instalments, VAT-compliant invoices and regional gateways for the UAE and Saudi Arabia, kit and grading fees on one family statement.
- **Parent app and coach app**: schedules, payments, absences, forms, progress reports, registers and session notes; WhatsApp and push reminders; progress/levels/gradings (swimming stages, martial-arts belts, football reports).
- **Camps, clinics and holiday courses**: single-day or weekly booking, before/after-care, medical forms, tablet check-in/out with authorised collectors.
- **Safeguarding and compliance**: coach vetting (DBS/background checks), first-aid and safeguarding certificate expiry, medical/consent records with role-based access, incident logs; GDPR and **COPPA** (US).
- **Multi-branch and franchise**: per-branch timetables/prices, one parent app, royalty/licence fee support, branch templates.

**Alternatives compared:** TeamSnap, PlayMetrics, Jackrabbit, ClassForKids, iClassPro, Amilia, regional white-label SaaS (Athleasy, Tamarran). See [Section 12](#12-own-vs-rent-alternatives-index).

### 5.4 Sports Club Mobile Apps

*Custom Sports Club App Development: a branded iOS and Android app your club owns.* Puts booking, payments, memberships and club news on members' phones.

- **Features**: book and pay, memberships and passes, open matches and community (leagues, results, rankings), push and WhatsApp notifications, QR pass that opens the door for the booked slot.
- **Ownership**: published under the **club's own Apple and Google developer accounts** (Apple $99/year, Google $25 one-off). Code repository accessible to the club; becomes the club's once paid in full. Member data in the club's own database.
- **Cross-platform**: one code base for iOS and Android (usually React Native or Flutter); same features also available on the web.
- **Built together**: app + website + staff admin delivered as one project, or the app can sit on top of an existing system that offers an interface.
- **White-label vs custom vs marketplace**: a decision guide is on the page, including the risk of white-label template apps being rejected under Apple App Store guidelines 4.2.6 and 4.3.
- **Building an "app like Playtomic"?** Scoped separately: a marketplace needs venue onboarding, matchmaking by level and location, payouts, commission/subscription model, reviews and support; usually started with one city and one sport.
- **Languages and payments**: English, Spanish, German, Italian and Arabic with full right-to-left layout; Stripe, Apple Pay, Google Pay, SEPA, Bizum, Gulf gateways.
- **After launch**: hosting, developer accounts and OS/store-rule updates; a support plan is offered after a fixed-price build.

- A **fixed quote for an agreed scope**, paid in **milestones**; price known before work starts.
